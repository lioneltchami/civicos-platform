# Independent Review — CivicOS GovStack Building Block Portfolio

## Conclusion

> **READY FOR HUMAN CLOSEOUT AND A SEPARATE PRODUCTION-PATH DECISION ONLY.**
>
> No Building Block is officially validated, certified, conformance-claimed, staging-authorised, release-ready, submission-ready, or submitted.

Two fresh independent reviews confirmed that the official specification and repository pins are valid, time-bounded source observations and that the local evidence statements remain conservative. Repository values are now explicitly labeled as observed `main`-tip SHAs at the 2026-08-22 retrieval time; they are not release pins, GitBook publication mappings, or conformance evidence.

| Review area | Conclusion |
|---|---|
| Official pins | Consent, Payments, Scheduler, and Content Management System specification/repository URLs and recorded observed main-tip SHAs were independently corroborated. |
| Official naming | The official catalog names **Content Management System** and does not list a distinct File Management Building Block. |
| Payments | RB-02 is internally closed only for its locked scope. The Option B artifact remains non-release; staging is deferred/not authorised. |
| Scheduler | SCH-01 and SCH-02.1 remain bounded internal closures; SCH-02.2 remains open/deferred. |
| Consent | Local evidence remains partial and cannot be generalized to the full official scope or live integration. |
| File Management | Local runner-enablement remains environment-blocked and cannot be represented as CMS conformance or a distinct official BB. |
| Portfolio non-claims | No certification, official validation, conformance, submission, release, production readiness, or staging authorisation is supported. |

The portfolio documents may be used by a human to close the current internal campaign and decide whether to open a new separately governed production-readiness path. That decision would need to inspect the underlying local artifacts and decision records; this review creates no operational authority.
